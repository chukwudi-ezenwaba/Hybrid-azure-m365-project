## Intune Components

1. **Device Enrollment** - BYOD + corporate devices
2. **Compliance Policies** - Password, encryption, software updates
3. **Configuration Profiles** - WiFi, VPN, email, security
4. **App Management** - Deploy M365 apps, block risky apps

## Execution Steps

### Week 21: Enroll Devices
- Communicate device enrollment to users (email + teams)
- Enable device enrollment (Company Portal)
- Enroll corporate-owned iOS/Android devices
- Verify enrollment > 80%

### Week 22: Policies & Compliance
- Deploy compliance policies (password, encryption)
- Deploy configuration profiles (WiFi, email)
- Monitor compliance (dashboard)
- Remediate non-compliant devices
